FAQ

Answers to the Questions
Every Serious Investor Asks

Can I set up a Saudi company without visiting Saudi Arabia?

Yes — entirely. Vision Gate KSA executes the full corporate formation process through official Saudi government portals: MISA investment license, Ministry of Commerce CR, ZATCA enrollment, GOSI registration, and all supporting portals. A Power of Attorney (POA), authenticated in your home country, is the only physical document required. Everything else is handled digitally on your behalf.

Can I own 100% of my Saudi company as a foreign national?

100% foreign ownership is permitted in the vast majority of commercial, industrial, and service sectors. Saudi Arabia abolished the mandatory Saudi partner requirement under the Foreign Investment Law, and the 2025 Investment Law further streamlined this. A narrow "negative list" (defence, certain media, properties in Makkah/Madinah) still applies. Our MISA framework cross-reference confirms your ceiling before any structure is proposed.

What is the minimum capital required for a foreign-owned company?

Under Saudi Arabia's 2025 investment reforms, most commercial, service, and technology sectors no longer require a minimum share capital for LLC formation. Regulated sectors such as banking, insurance, and financial services retain sector-specific requirements — some exceeding SAR 5 million. Our advisory system cross-references the MISA Activity framework to provide your exact figure before any commitment.

How does the 2026 Foreign Real Estate Ownership Law work for non-residents?

Under Royal Decree M/14 (effective 22 January 2026), non-resident international buyers can purchase property in REGA-approved geographic zones without prior Saudi presence. Steps: (1) obtain a Digital ID via a Saudi embassy abroad, (2) authenticate via Nafath, (3) select property on the Saudi Properties platform, (4) pay ~10% transaction fees (5% RETT + REGA disposal fee), (5) REGA issues an electronic title deed. Violations carry fines up to SAR 10 million.

Does buying property automatically give me Premium Residency?

Not automatically — but it creates direct eligibility. Under the Real Estate Owner PRP track, you qualify only if you own a mortgage-free residential property valued at SAR 4,000,000 or above. A PRP application must still be formally submitted and approved by the Saudi Premium Residency Center (SPRC). Properties below SAR 4M, with active mortgages, or in commercial categories do not qualify. Our partner network handles both acquisition and PRP as a unified engagement.

What is the difference between Limited Duration and Unlimited Duration PRP?

Limited Duration PRP costs SAR 100,000/year, renewable annually — ideal for HNWIs wanting flexible self-sponsored presence. Unlimited Duration PRP is a one-time SAR 800,000 fee granting permanent, lifelong residency with no renewal, full work rights, and family inclusion — Saudi Arabia's equivalent of a Golden Visa. The five category-based tracks (RE Owner, Investor, Entrepreneur, Special Talent, Gifted) cost just SAR 4,000 each for a 5-year renewable permit.
